How it works

The big picture and the next step, in one place.

Real screens from KanKan on iPhone. One project, followed from start to finish.

  1. Start on one board.

    Your work moves through To Do, Doing and Done. A card that holds a board shows its progress, here 2 of 4.

  2. Open the card.

    Due date, reminders, notes and a checklist stay with the work. Its status follows its board.

  3. Work on its board.

    The project gets its own To Do, Doing and Done. Break the work down as far as you need.

  4. Progress rolls up.

    When the last card on its board is done, the project moves to Done by itself.

Boards inside boards.

A card on a project board can hold a board too. Here, Shoot product photos sits inside the bakery project. A breadcrumb shows the path back to My Board, so you never get lost.

Activity log

Pick up where you left off.

Every card keeps a dated log, newest first. Forgot where you were on a project? Open the card and read the last things you did.

  • Write what you did. Type a line in the card’s Activity box. You can edit or delete your own lines later.
  • Moves are noted for you. When you move the card to another column, KanKan adds a line.
  • So are checklist ticks. Tick or untick an item, and KanKan adds a line.

Nothing else adds lines. A project that moves to Done because its board is finished does not add one. The log stays with the card when you archive, restore or export it.

What you get

Simple at first. Deep when you need it.

  • Boards inside cards

    Any card can hold its own board, and those cards can hold boards too. On deeper boards, a breadcrumb shows the path.

  • Progress that rolls up

    A card with a board shows how much of that board is done. Reopen work, and the parent moves back too.

  • Activity log

    Each card keeps a dated log, newest first. Write what you did. Column moves and checklist ticks are added for you.

  • Notes, checklists, due dates

    Keep the details with the work. A checklist item can become a card when it needs more room.

  • Reminders on iPhone

    Get a reminder before work is due, and an optional daily summary of what is overdue or due today.

  • Tags and Focus

    Tag your cards. Turn on Focus to show only the work you choose, on every board.

  • Archive and export

    Archive finished work and restore it later, with everything inside it. Export a backup at any time.

  • iPhone and Mac

    The same boards on both. Turn on iCloud sync to keep them in step. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Agent Access on Mac

    Optional. Let tools you trust on the same Mac read and edit your boards. You can revoke access at any time.
