The problem

Most projects take more than one sitting.

You start something, and then the day ends or something else comes up. A week later, you have to work out where you were.

In KanKan, each project is a card. Move it to Doing while you work on it. Everything else waits in To Do. When it is done, move it to Done.

  1. See what you started.

    Doing shows only the projects in progress. To Do can be as long as it needs to be.

  2. Open the card.

    The due date, tags, notes and checklist stay with the work.

Activity log

Every card remembers what you did.

Each card keeps a dated log, newest first. Come back after a day or a month, open the card and read your last steps. Then do the next one.

  • Write what you did. Type a line in the card’s Activity box. You can edit or delete your own lines later.
  • Moves and ticks are noted for you. When you move the card to another column, or tick a checklist item, KanKan adds a line.
  • Assistant changes show a name. When an AI assistant changes a card, the line shows the assistant’s name.
  • Nothing gets forgotten. The Today screen lists cards in Doing that have had no activity for more than 7 days.

The log stays with the card when you archive, restore or export it.

AI assistants, on Mac

Let your assistant knock off some to-dos.

With Agent Access, AI tools that run on your Mac can work on your boards. For example Claude, ChatGPT and Grok, or coding tools like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. Ask one to add the steps of a project, sort a board or tick off what it finished.

  • You choose the access

    Create a token for each assistant in Settings. Read-only tokens can look. Read-and-write tokens can add and edit cards, checklists and due dates, and create and arrange boards, columns and tags. Revoke a token at any time.

  • Deleting asks you first

    Before an assistant deletes anything, or changes more than 10 cards at once, it must ask you in its chat. Nothing changes until you say yes. This is on by default.

  • You see who did what

    When an assistant changes a card, the card’s activity log shows its name. Assistants cannot edit or delete your log lines.

  • On your Mac, while KanKan is open

    The connection stays on your Mac. When KanKan is closed or your Mac is asleep or off, assistants cannot reach your boards. They cannot connect from your iPhone or from the internet.

Agent Access is optional, and off until you create a token. It sends nothing to DECEMBER CAPITAL. How Agent Access works

For bigger projects

Too big for one card? Give it its own board.

Boards inside cards

Any card can hold its own board with To Do, Doing and Done. The card takes its status from that board, and moves to Done by itself when the work inside is done.

Tags and Focus

Focus only on the cards you need.

Tag cards personal, work, family or vacation. Turn on Focus and tick the tags you want. Every board then shows only the cards with one of those tags, including the boards inside cards.

  • Tick one tag or several. The Focus menu in the toolbar is a checkbox list of your tags, with “No Tags” and “Select All”.
  • You always know it’s on. A Focus chip stays at the top until you clear it.
  • Tags can nest. Tick work, and cards tagged work/marketing show too.

What else you get

Everything a project needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

  • Due dates and reminders

    Give cards and checklist items a due date. On iPhone, you get a reminder the day before and one at the due time by default. Change the defaults in Settings, or set different reminders for one card.

  • Today screen

    One list of what is overdue, due today and coming up this week, plus forgotten cards in Doing. On iPhone, you can also get a daily summary each morning.

  • Checklists

    Break a card into steps and tick them off. Ticked items move to the bottom, so the next step stays on top. When a step grows, turn it into a card on the card’s own board.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Ask Siri or use the Shortcuts app to add a card, see what is due today, move a card or open a board.

  • iPhone and Mac

    Use KanKan on both. Turn on iCloud sync to keep the same boards on iPhone and Mac, in your own private iCloud. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Archive and export

    Archive finished work and restore it later, with everything inside it. Export a backup at any time.
