How it works

The big picture and the next step, in one place.

  1. Start with cards.

    Add a card in seconds. Move it from To Do to Doing to Done. Simple work stays simple.

  2. Give a card a board.

    When a card turns into a project, add a board to it. Boards can sit inside boards, as deep as you need.

  3. Progress rolls up.

    A card with a board takes its status from the work inside. When the last card there is done, the parent moves to Done by itself.

What you get

Simple at first. Deep when you need it.

  • Boards inside cards

    Any card can hold its own board. A path at the top shows where you are, so you never get lost.

  • Progress that rolls up

    A card with a board shows how much of that board is done. Reopen work, and the parent moves back too.

  • Notes, checklists, due dates

    Keep the details with the work. A checklist item can become a card when it needs more room.

  • Reminders on iPhone

    Get a reminder before work is due, and an optional daily summary of what is overdue or due today.

  • Tags and Focus

    Tag your cards. Turn on Focus to show only the work you choose, on every board.

  • Archive and export

    Archive finished work and restore it later, with everything inside it. Export a backup at any time.

  • iPhone and Mac

    The same boards on both. Turn on iCloud sync to keep them in step. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Agent Access on Mac

    Optional. Let tools you trust on the same Mac read and edit your boards. You can revoke access at any time.

Privacy
