Personal Kanban for iPhone and Mac

You’re not actually doing everything on your to-do list.

Leave to-dos in To Do, and move to Doing what you need to work on now.

KanKan is a personal Kanban app for iPhone and Mac. Your Doing column stays short, so you always know what you are working on now. And each card’s activity log shows what you did last.

How it works

Keep your big to-do list in its own column.

Move to Doing only what you’re actively working on now.

When it’s done, move it to Done. Archive it whenever you want. Not the right time after all? Move the card back to To Do.

  1. Move only what you’re doing.

    Drag a card to Doing when you start it. Everything else waits in To Do, so Doing shows what you are working on now.

  2. Open the card.

    A due date with reminders, tags, a note and a checklist. The details stay with the work.

3Activity log

Pick up where you left off.

Tick things off and note what you did. Every card keeps a dated log, newest first. Forgot where you were? Open the card and read your last steps.

  • Write what you did. Type a line in the card’s Activity box. You can edit or delete your own lines later.
  • Moves are noted for you. When you move the card to another column, KanKan adds a line.
  • So are checklist ticks. Tick or untick an item, and KanKan adds a line.

Nothing else adds lines. A project that moves to Done because its board is finished does not add one. The log stays with the card when you archive, restore or export it.

Focus

Focus only on the cards you need.

Tag cards personal, work, family or vacation. Turn on Focus for one tag, and every board shows only the cards with that tag, including the boards inside cards.

  • Set it from any board. Pick a tag from the Focus menu in the toolbar.
  • You always know it’s on. A “Focus: home” chip stays at the top until you clear it.
  • Tags can nest. Focus on work, and cards tagged work/marketing show too.

What you get

Everything a card needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

  • Activity log

    Each card keeps a dated log, newest first. Write what you did. Column moves and checklist ticks are added for you.

  • Due dates and reminders

    Give cards and checklist items a due date. On iPhone, you get a reminder the day before and one at the due time by default; change this in Settings. Plus an optional daily summary.

  • Tags

    Tag cards by what they are about, like family or home. Tags can nest, like work/marketing.

  • Checklists

    Break a card into steps and tick them off. When a step grows, turn it into a card on the card’s own board.

For bigger work

Too big for a to-do list? Give it its own board.

Open Plan the garden’s board

Boards inside cards

Any card can hold its own board with To Do, Doing and Done. The card takes its status from that board, and moves to Done by itself when the work inside is done.

  • Agent Access on Mac

    Optional. Tools you trust on the same Mac can read and update your cards. They cannot create boards, columns or tags. You can revoke access at any time.

  • iPhone and Mac

    Use KanKan on both. Turn on iCloud sync to keep the same boards on iPhone and Mac, in your own private iCloud. It is off until you turn it on.

  • Archive and export

    Archive finished work and restore it later, with everything inside it. Export a backup at any time.

Privacy

Your boards are yours.

Your boards stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud. DECEMBER CAPITAL cannot see them. An assistant can read them only if you give it access on your Mac.
