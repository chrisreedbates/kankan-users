KanKan

A Kanban board where every card can hold its own board.

Start with simple cards. When a card grows into a project, give it a board. Do this as deep as you need.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone and Mac.

What you get

  • Boards inside cards. A card with a board gets its status from the work inside it.
  • iPhone and Mac. Turn on iCloud sync to keep both in step.
  • Private by design. Your boards stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. DECEMBER CAPITAL cannot see them. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Reminders. Due dates, reminders and the daily summary are scheduled on your device.
